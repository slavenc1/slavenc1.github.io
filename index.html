01 / Research approach

Three ways to
understand a river.

Monitoring, modelling and laboratory work connect observations with physical processes and engineering decisions.

01

Monitoring

Measuring sediment transport and reservoir changes using hydroacoustics, sediment sampling, bathymetry and optical observations.

  • Statkraft R&D — Devoll & ChevesDigitalisation and sediment monitoring in hydropower.
  • ADCPx / ADCP-RiverSedBedload measurements and acoustic data processing in large rivers.
  • HYPOSSuspended sediment monitoring with satellite observations and optical sensors.
02

Modelling

1D and 2D hydraulic and sediment modelling, including long-term predictions of sediment transport and reservoir sedimentation.

  • Statkraft R&DReservoir sedimentation modelling and long-term predictions.
  • Vågåmo flood protectionHydraulic and sediment analysis for flood mitigation and tunnel intakes.
  • NATURE-DEMOFlood, erosion and sediment resilience with nature-based solutions.
03

Laboratory work

Controlled experiments on bedload transport, acoustic measurements and the hydraulic forces governing boulder motion.

  • SEDIPASS / doctoral researchLaboratory investigation of apparent bedload velocity measured by ADCPs.
  • Acoustic measurement methodsBistatic and monostatic configurations over moving sediment beds.
  • Boulder stability with Jan HrebrinaIncipient motion, drag and lift under different flow conditions.

03 / Selected experience

Selected projects.

Field evidence and numerical analysis inform practical solutions for sediment management and flood resilience.

01

Hydroacoustics / Germany

ADCPx / ADCP-RiverSed

Developing acoustic methods and processing software for bedload monitoring in large rivers, combining ADCP measurements, field campaigns and data analysis to improve estimates of sediment transport under different flow conditions.

Research, field campaigns and software development.

ADCP-RiverSed · GitHub ↗
02

Statkraft R&D / Albania & Peru

Sediments in hydropower

Digitalisation and sediment monitoring at Devoll and Cheves hydropower schemes, combining instrumentation, bathymetric surveys, sediment sampling and data analysis with 1D and 2D modelling for long-term predictions of reservoir sedimentation.

Sediment specialist and technical lead.

03

Flood resilience / Norway

Vågåmo flood protection

Assessing sediment transport and hydraulic conditions to support flood protection in Vågåmo, using two-dimensional modelling to investigate sediment deposition, conveyance capacity and the performance of proposed flood tunnel intake arrangements.

Hydrologist and hydraulic engineer.

Vågåmo flood protection · Vågå kommune ↗
04

Nature-based solutions / Europe

NATURE-DEMO

Investigating nature-based solutions for infrastructure resilience to floods, erosion and sedimentation, connecting hydraulic expertise with assessment methods and interdisciplinary research to support the planning and evaluation of practical mitigation measures.

Hydraulic expert and research contributor.

NATURE-DEMO website ↗
05

Doctoral research / Hydropower

SEDIPASS

Investigating sediment challenges in hydropower through field measurements, laboratory experiments and numerical analysis, with research on bedload monitoring, acoustic measurement techniques, sediment yield and the implications of sediment for operations.

Doctoral researcher in monitoring and modelling.

SediPASS · NTNU HydroCen ↗
06

Horizon 2020 / Remote sensing

HYPOS

Investigating suspended sediment monitoring using satellite observations and optical sensors, combining remote sensing with field data and sediment analysis to support the assessment of sediment conditions in rivers and reservoirs.

Researcher in monitoring and data analysis.

HYPOS project · EOMAP ↗
